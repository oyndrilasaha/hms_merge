Specializations & Clinical Services

Comprehensive specialized medicine and interdisciplinary teams supporting patients at every stage of recovery.

Cardiology & Cardiothoracic Surgery

State-of-the-art cardiac catheterization laboratories, coronary care units, pacemaker implantation, and complex heart valve surgeries.

  • Open Heart Unit
  • Echocardiography
  • 24/7 STEMI Protocol

Emergency Medicine & Level 1 Trauma

Designated major adult trauma referral centre equipped with advanced resuscitation bays, dedicated trauma teams, and rapid surgical access.

  • 24/7 Fast Triage
  • Helipad Access
  • Major Trauma Team

Oncology & Clinical Hematology

Holistic cancer care comprising medical oncology, targeted chemotherapy infusions, radiation oncology, hematological stem cell therapy, and trials.

  • Infusion Suites
  • Clinical Trials
  • Multidisciplinary Care

Orthopedics & Musculoskeletal Rehab

Pioneering robotic joint replacement surgery, spinal trauma reconstruction, pediatric orthopedic clinics, and dedicated physiotherapy wards.

  • Joint Replacement
  • Sports Surgery
  • Fast-Track Rehab

Maternity & Women's Health

Modern birthing suites, maternal-fetal medicine clinics, special care nursery for neonates, and comprehensive post-natal support services.

  • Private Birthing Suites
  • Neonatal Special Care
  • Prenatal Education

Neurology & Neurosurgery

Nationally recognized acute stroke unit with endovascular thrombectomy, video EEG epilepsy monitoring, and brain tumor microsurgery.

  • Rapid Stroke Unit
  • Epilepsy Monitoring
  • Stereotactic Surgery

Diagnostic Pathology & Laboratory

NATA-accredited 24-hour pathology laboratory executing full-spectrum hematology, clinical biochemistry, microbiology, and molecular genetics.

  • NATA Accredited
  • Digital Results Sync
  • Rapid Blood Bank

Clinical Pharmacy & Therapeutics

Specialist hospital pharmacy providing sterile chemotherapy compounding, clinical pharmacist ward consultations, and patient discharge counseling.

  • Specialist Compounding
  • Discharge Counseling
  • Barcode Dispensing

Patient Experience

Hospital Perks & Modern Amenities

Designed for comfort, accessibility, and healing for patients, families, and visiting carers.

Transport

Direct Public Transit Access

Only a 400m sheltered walk from Kogarah Railway Station with frequent express trains to Sydney CBD, plus 5 connecting bus routes at our front entrance.

Parking

Multi-Storey On-Site Parking

Safe underground parking with 800+ spaces, electric vehicle charging bays, direct elevator access to all wards, and subsidized patient concession rates.

Digital Health

Integrated Patient Portal & PWA

Access your synthetic lab reports, digital tax invoices, scheduled clinical notes, and CSV records from any desktop or mobile device 24/7.

Rehabilitation

Hydrotherapy & Wellness Suites

Heated therapeutic hydrotherapy pool and state-of-the-art physiotherapy gym for post-operative recovery, musculoskeletal rehab, and mobility restoration.

Environment

Healing Gardens & Modern Cafeteria

Landscaped open-air healing gardens, private quiet reflection rooms, and a modern cafeteria offering healthy, chef-prepared dietary meals and barista coffee.

Support

24/7 Language & Cultural Care

Accredited multi-lingual medical interpreters available in 40+ languages, along with dedicated Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander health liaison officers.
